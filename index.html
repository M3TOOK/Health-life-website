Description

Healthy Life app is a program that aims to raise health awareness and help people become healthier through healthy habits, the best exercise plan, and the best personalized diet plan.

Goals

  • Better healthy life
  • Spreading awareness in the field of health
  • Facilitating health care

Features

  • Did all the health-related accounts
  • Make the best meal plan
  • Make the best training plan
  • Make the best plan to track healthy habits

Food in health life app

  1. Enter information about user like current weight, height, age, Gender and activity level
  2. Enter Target
  3. Enter health problems the user suffers from
  4. Calculate Calories and Macros
  5. Make a best diet plane for user

Sports in health life app

  1. Make a best sports plane for user Through body measure
  2. Distribution of training and rest days throughout the week

Habits in health life app

  1. Enter user interests
  2. Through user interests Create best daily, Weekly and monthly habits for user
